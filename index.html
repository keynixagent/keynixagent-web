Fair questions

Before you message us

We only have phone footage. Is that enough?

Yes. Most great reels start on a phone. We pick the moments, fix the colour and pacing, and fill the gaps where a shot is missing.

Will people notice the AI?

That's the point: they shouldn't. AI is a garnish, not the meal. We use it for one or two shots your camera couldn't get, and grade it to match the rest.

What if I don't like it?

Then you don't pay for the first reel. After that it's month to month, with 30 days' notice. No contracts that outlive the season.

How fast?

24 to 48 hours per reel once we have your footage and a short brief.

Why should I trust a new studio?

You shouldn't, yet. That's why the first reel is on us if it doesn't work for you, and why the teardown is free.
